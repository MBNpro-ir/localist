# Localist privacy policy

Localist is developed by PRS. Contact the project at [PRS Hub](https://prshub.ir/) or report a privacy issue through the [Localist repository](https://github.com/MBNpro-ir/localist).

Localist transfers the selected files, text, video, screen or audio to the device you choose. Quick Send discovery exposes your assigned device name and availability on the local network. Received files, transfer history, saved devices and preferences are stored on your device. Use transfer history to open or share a received file. Clearing history removes its records. Delete a file explicitly to remove the file itself.

Quick Send, Stream and Camera & Mic communicate over the local network. Quick Send uses protected transfer by default, with optional legacy compatibility. Stream and the Apple browser transfer use local HTTP, so use them on a trusted network. Camera & Mic uses encrypted WebRTC media and local connection discovery. Camera and microphone recording start when you select Start. Stop capture in Localist or from its Android service notification. Android screen and playback capture use the system's consent flow.

Remote Control uses RustDesk. The selected ID and relay service receive the metadata needed to locate and connect devices, including connection identifiers and network addresses. A relay can carry remote session traffic when required. You can choose the RustDesk server in Settings. Android supports viewing the hosted screen after system consent and controlling supported remote devices from the phone. If you enable Android Accessibility for remote input, Localist applies the other device's gestures and text commands during a remote session you authorize. The other person can interact with apps on the screen while control is active. You can disable it in Accessibility settings.

The Android proxy feature uses the explicit local HTTP or SOCKS5 endpoints you select. It does not create an Android system VPN. On Windows and direct-distribution Android builds, Localist checks for releases at `mbnpro.ir`. Windows downloads the Setup package from the same host. On Android, the download button opens the hosted APK in your browser. A Google Play distribution build uses Google Play for updates.

Diagnostic reports are stored locally. If you choose to share a report, review it before sending. Localist has no in-app advertising or analytics account. The project sites and GitHub are separate services opened when you choose their links.
